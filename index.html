Privacy Summary: Earth Scope respects your privacy. All saved map location pins, attached photos, recorded speedometer trips, and location history are stored locally on your device. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Earth Scope ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how the Earth Scope mobile application (Package ID: com.shafaqat.earthscope), developed by Shafaqat, accesses, uses, and safeguards information when you use our interactive 3D World Globe, Google Maps with Custom Floating Pins, Live GPS Speedometer, Digital Compass, Altitude HUD, Nearby Places Finder, and World Country Atlas features.

By installing or using Earth Scope, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy and our Terms & Conditions of Use.

2. Permissions & Data We Access

To provide core navigation, mapping, and geographic tools, Earth Scope requests only the minimum required Android permissions:

  • Location Permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Used only while the app is open in the foreground to display your live GPS position on the 3D Globe and Google Map, calculate real-time driving/walking speed in the Speedometer, measure GPS altitude and coordinates, find nearby places around you, and allow you to save or share your current location pin. Your live GPS coordinates are processed directly on your device and are never uploaded to any external tracking server owned by us.
  • Photos & Media Selection (Android Zero-Permission Photo Picker): When creating or editing a saved Floating Location Pin on the map, you may optionally attach up to 3 photos. Earth Scope uses Android's official system PickVisualMedia Photo Picker, which lets you select specific images without granting broad storage access to your entire photo gallery. Selected photos are copied privately into the app's internal local storage on your device.
  • Device Compass & Motion Sensors: Earth Scope reads your phone's built-in rotation vector, accelerometer, and magnetometer sensors in real time to orient the 3D Globe and Standalone Digital Compass (North, South, East, West). Sensor data is processed transiently in memory and is never stored or transmitted.
  • Internet & Network Access (INTERNET): Used to load interactive map tiles (Google Maps / Leaflet map layers), reverse-geocode coordinates into readable street/city names, and load clearly labeled advertisements via Google AdMob.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): Used for optional haptic feedback and speed limit alerts in the Speedometer.

3. Local Data Storage & User Control (Room Database)

All user-created content—including Saved Floating Map Pins, custom marker styles, categories (Home, Office, Shop, Property, Travel), notes, attached pin photos, Saved Places, Recorded Speedometer Trips, Searched Country History, and theme settings (White Theme, Dark Space, or System Default)—is stored locally on your device using Android's Room SQLite Database and SharedPreferences.

  • You have 100% control over your stored data at all times.
  • You can edit or delete any individual saved pin, photo, trip log, or history entry directly inside the app.
  • Uninstalling the application or clearing the app's storage from Android Settings permanently removes all locally stored records from your device.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertisements

Earth Scope integrates trusted third-party SDKs to provide mapping and keep the application free for all users:

  • Google Maps SDK for Android / Google Play Services: Used to render interactive road, satellite, terrain, and stylized maps. Use of Google Maps is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdMob (Advertisements): Earth Scope displays clearly labeled advertisements (Adaptive Banners, Native Ad Cards, and frequency-capped Interstitial Ads). Google AdMob may collect and use your device's Advertising ID (AAID), coarse device diagnostics, and ad interaction data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads in compliance with Google Play policies. You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time in your Android device settings under Settings → Google → Ads.

5. Data Security

We value your trust and prioritize security. All network communication performed by third-party SDKs (such as Google Maps and Google AdMob) is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections, and all personal pins, notes, and photos remain sandboxed within your device's private application storage.

6. Children's Privacy

Earth Scope is a general-audience geographic, educational, and navigation utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

7. Terms & Conditions of Use

  • Road & Driving Safety Warning: Always pay full attention to the road and obey local traffic laws while driving, cycling, or walking. Never type notes, pick photos, or interact with complex map controls while operating a moving vehicle.
  • Informational Accuracy Disclaimer: GPS speed, altitude, compass heading, and geodesic capital-to-capital distances depend on your device's hardware sensors and satellite signal reception. Measurements are provided for general reference and educational purposes only and should not be relied upon for aviation, marine, or life-critical navigation.
